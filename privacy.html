Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

This policy explains what information Open Sky collects when you visit this website and how it is used. In short: visitors do not create accounts, the site has no advertising or tracking, and I do not sell personal information.

What the site collects

I do not ask visitors for any personal information. There are no sign-ups, comments or contact forms.

As with any website, the services that deliver Open Sky receive technical information whenever your browser requests a page or a photograph: your IP address, browser and device type, the address requested and the time. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and fix faults. I do not use it to identify you.

Services that run this site

  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Supabase stores the photographs with their titles, descriptions and dates, and delivers the images.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser downloads them from Google, so Google receives your IP address.
  • jsDelivr delivers a code library the site needs to load photographs.

Each provider handles technical data under its own privacy policy.

Cookies and analytics

The public pages do not set cookies and do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. The private admin area, which only I can use, keeps a sign-in session in my own browser.

Information in photographs

Each photograph has a title, description, date and category, and sometimes a place name, all chosen by me. Photographs are resized and re-saved before they are uploaded, which removes camera data such as GPS coordinates stored in the original file.

Sharing

I do not sell or rent information about visitors. Technical data is handled only by the providers listed above, or disclosed if the law requires it.

How long information is kept

Server logs are kept by the providers above for the periods set in their own policies. Photographs and their details stay on the site until I remove them.

Children

This site is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to see or delete personal information, can be sent to . If you appear in a photograph and want it removed, email me and I will take it down.
