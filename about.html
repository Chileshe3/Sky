About

Open Sky is a small, personal photography magazine of skies, light and the land beneath them.

I photograph sunsets and sunrises, cloud formations, the moon and the night sky, and the quiet landscapes and plants I come across. Each photograph is published when I take it, so the gallery grows a little at a time.

You can browse everything in the gallery, or pick a category from the menu.
